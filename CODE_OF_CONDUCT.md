# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome in this project, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Our standards

Please be kind and respectful. Accept feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project, and show empathy towards others. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code. To report a problem, write to the corresponding author, Anwar P. P. Abdul Majeed (anwarm@sunway.edu.my). All reports are handled in confidence.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
